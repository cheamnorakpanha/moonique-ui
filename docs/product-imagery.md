# Product imagery

OpenAI’s built-in image generation tool created the ten catalog illustrations below. The storefront serves the optimized WebP files from `public/images/products` and labels them **Illustrative photo**. Keep `imageIsIllustrative` enabled until a product has approved shop photography.

The images show regional examples and illustrative serving choices. Naem uses the Battambang fish-parcel style, Ph’ak uses paork-chav-style fermented fish pieces, and Special Fried Sticky Rice is shown as pressed golden rice cakes. The shop’s specific preparations and portions await confirmation.

## Prompt set

Each generation used the corresponding dish prompt followed by this shared direction.

> Photorealistic editorial food photography for a Cambodian artisan storefront. Single landscape 3:2 frame, overhead at a slight angle, whole subject grouped inside the central 80% for product-card cropping. Warm diffused window light, rich natural food texture, warm dark wood, restrained rustic Cambodian tableware, earthy olive and brown tones. One dish only. No lettering, logos, labels, people or watermark.
